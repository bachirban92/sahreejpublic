Delete your Sahreej account

Request deletion in the app

Open Account → Settings → Delete account. Sahreej records your request and shows its status.

Customers

Complete or cancel any active delivery before requesting account deletion.

Drivers

Complete any active delivery first. If settlement is still due, the deletion request may remain pending until the balance is cleared.

What is deleted

Once the request is eligible for completion, Sahreej deletes or anonymizes personal data that is no longer required, including saved places, notification subscriptions, and unnecessary profile data.

What may be retained

Some order, settlement, dispute, fraud-prevention, or legal/accounting records may need to be retained for a required period. Where practical, identifying information is minimized or anonymized.
