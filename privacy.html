Sahreej Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Sahreej connects customers requesting water tanker delivery with approved tanker drivers. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Sahreej and how it is used.

Information we process

Depending on how you use Sahreej, we may process your name, mobile number, delivery address and location, saved delivery places, delivery instructions, order history, support messages, notification identifiers, and account-deletion requests.

For drivers, Sahreej may also process driver and tanker photos, vehicle information, verification documents, availability and location while online or completing a delivery, delivery history, earnings, and settlement records.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, match customers with eligible drivers, fulfill and track deliveries, provide customer support, operate driver approval and settlements, prevent abuse, send service notifications, and maintain required operational or accounting records.

Location

Customer location is used to select and fulfill a delivery location. Driver location is used for availability, dispatch, navigation, arrival verification, and active delivery tracking.

Service providers

Sahreej uses third-party service providers for hosting, database and authentication services, mapping and routing, notifications, and other infrastructure needed to operate the service. SMS verification providers may also be used when production phone verification is enabled.

Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for delivery operations, support, fraud prevention, accounting or legal obligations, and account-deletion compliance. Where possible, precise tracking and diagnostic data is retained for shorter periods than order, settlement, dispute, or accounting records.

Account deletion

Users can request account deletion from Sahreej settings. Active deliveries may need to be completed or cancelled first. Driver deletion requests may remain pending until outstanding settlement obligations are cleared. Data that no longer needs to be retained will be deleted or anonymized when the request is completed.

Security

Sahreej uses encrypted network connections, access controls, private document storage, database row-level security, and server-side authorization checks. No system can guarantee absolute security.
